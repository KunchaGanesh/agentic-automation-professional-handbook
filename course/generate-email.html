Generate the Decision Email

⚠️ Use the settlement result
The Reviewer Action Gateway runs before settlement, so it keeps vars.action == "Approve". In Generate Decision Email also check the coded agent's result: use the approval email only when vars.action == "Approve" && vars.eligible == true; otherwise generate the additional-document or not-approved email. Set Output format to HTML and use <p> and <br> tags — never literal \n.

Both branches meet at Generate Approval Email. A single GenAI activity writes either the approval email or the "more documents needed" email, depending on vars.action.

68

Choose the Generate Email activity

Select the task Generate Approval Email. Set Action to Execute connector activity, then click Select activity.

Search for generate email and pick Generate Email from UiPath GenAI Activities — not one of the Gmail, Outlook, Mailjet or SES "Send Email" activities.

Selected Generate Email from UiPath GenAI Activities.

Configure the activity

Set the properties below, then click the Email content field to open the text builder.

Paste the expression below into the Email content text builder. It branches on vars.action: the first half builds the approval email, the second half the rejection / more documents email (anything else returns an "Invalid action" message).

Email content (JS expression)
vars.action === "Approve"
? [
    "Write a professional HTML email to the customer confirming that their insurance claim has been approved following supervisor review. Use only <p>, <ul>, <li>, <strong>, and <br> HTML tags.",
    "<p>Dear ", vars.outClaimDetails.customer_name, ",</p>",
    "<p>We are writing regarding your claim with the following details:</p>",
    "<ul>",
    "<li><strong>Customer name:</strong> ", vars.outClaimDetails.customer_name, "</li>",
    "<li><strong>Claim ID:</strong> ", vars.outClaimDetails.claim_id, "</li>",
    "<li><strong>Policy number:</strong> ", vars.outClaimDetails.policy_number, "</li>",
    "</ul>",
    "<p>Following supervisor review, your claim has been <strong>approved</strong>.</p>",
    "<p>The settlement details are as follows:</p>",
    "<ul>",
    "<li><strong>Original claim amount:</strong> ", vars.outClaimDetails.claim_amount, "</li>",
    "<li><strong>Settlement percentage:</strong> ", vars.settlementPercentage, "%</li>",
    "<li><strong>Settlement amount:</strong> ", vars.settlementAmount, "</li>",
    "</ul>",
    "Rewrite the following supervisor comments in polite, clear, customer-friendly language. Add them as a short paragraph titled Reviewer notes after the settlement details. Preserve the meaning, correct grammar, and do not mention fraud scores, fraud indicators, or internal system details: ",
    vars.outReviewerComments,
    "<p>If you have any questions regarding this outcome or your policy coverage, please contact our helpline using the number below.</p>",
    "<p>Regards,<br>Claims Processing Team<br>Insurance Claims Department<br>Helpline: 1800-CLAIMS</p>"
].join("")
: vars.action === "Reject"
? [
    "Write a professional HTML email to the customer explaining that their insurance claim could not be approved as submitted and that additional documentation is required. Use only <p>, <ul>, <li>, <strong>, and <br> HTML tags.",
    "<p>Dear ", vars.outClaimDetails.customer_name, ",</p>",
    "<p>We are writing regarding your insurance claim with the following details:</p>",
    "<ul>",
    "<li><strong>Claim ID:</strong> ", vars.outClaimDetails.claim_id, "</li>",
    "<li><strong>Policy number:</strong> ", vars.outClaimDetails.policy_number, "</li>",
    "</ul>",
    "<p>After a thorough review, we are unable to approve your claim as submitted. At this stage, we require additional documentation and clarification before we can continue our assessment.</p>",
    "Rewrite the following supervisor comments in polite, clear, customer-friendly language. Add them as a short paragraph titled Reason for decision after the paragraph above. Preserve the meaning, correct grammar, and do not mention fraud scores, fraud indicators, or internal system details: ",
    vars.outReviewerComments,
    "<p>Please resubmit your claim with the following documents and information:</p>",
    "<ul>",
    "<li>Detailed incident description, including date, time, location, and sequence of events</li>",
    "<li>Supporting evidence, such as photos, repair estimates, invoices, or receipts</li>",
    "<li>Any relevant police, incident, or official reports</li>",
    "<li>Proof of ownership or value for the items or property involved</li>",
    "<li>Any prior correspondence related to this incident or similar claims</li>",
    "</ul>",
    "<p>When resubmitting, clearly include your Claim ID: <strong>", vars.outClaimDetails.claim_id, "</strong> on all documents and communications.</p>",
    "<p>Resubmitting the requested information will allow us to conduct a further review; however, it does not guarantee that the claim will be approved.</p>",
    "<p>Sincerely,<br>Insurance Claims Department<br>Helpline: 1800-CLAIMS</p>"
].join("")
: "<p>Invalid action. Please select Approve or Reject.</p>"
⚠️ Check the variable chips
After pasting, every vars.… reference should turn into a blue chip. The claim fields come from vars.outClaimDetails. If vars.settlementPercentage, vars.settlementAmount or vars.outReviewerComments does not resolve, delete it and use Insert variable to pick the settlement agent outputs and the supervisor's Reviewer comments.

Finally, scroll down and confirm Output format is HTML and Style is Concise.
